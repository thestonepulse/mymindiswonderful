Why this exists

I wanted somewhere to put the things I learn, the things I notice, and the questions I can't stop thinking about.

and leave the door open for anyone curious enough to explore them.

A different kind of archive

This is not meant to be a feed. There is no pressure to post, perform or keep up.

It is closer to an old naturalist's notebook: imperfect, growing, full of references, observations and questions. Some pages will contain established evidence. Others will contain history, interpretation or personal reflection. The distinction matters.

The aim is simple: curiosity → exploration → learning → reflection.

How knowledge is handled

Evidence

Claims grounded in research or reliable primary sources.

Historical knowledge

Documented knowledge carried through communities and generations.

Observation

Things personally noticed, recorded and clearly labelled.

Interpretation

Ideas formed by connecting evidence, history and experience.

Speculation

Possibilities that are interesting precisely because they are not established.
