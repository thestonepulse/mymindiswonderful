The habit of looking twice

I went outside expecting to notice something dramatic. Instead, I noticed the same patch of ground I had walked past dozens of times — only this time I stopped.

There were tiny leaves, a line of ants, a piece of lichen, moisture under a stone. The lesson was embarrassingly simple: attention changes the world without changing the world.
